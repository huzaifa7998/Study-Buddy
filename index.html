How the matching works

📚 Shared Subjects50%
🗓 Schedule Overlap30%
🤝 Study Style20%

When you set up your profile, the engine compares you against every other student in your department and semester and computes a compatibility score out of 100.

Subject overlap is the biggest predictor — studying OS together only works if you're both enrolled in it.

Style & schedule ensure the match is practical: a night-owl who loves group debates won't mesh with a morning solo-studier.
